**Week:** 31 August – 6 September 2026  
**Phase:** F0 Baseline  
**Goal:** Build a first AI consultant baseline, establish the working environment, and create the initial portfolio structure.

## 1. What I learned

During Week 1, I focused on building a clear baseline across AI concepts, solution selection, Git/GitHub, and AI consulting thinking.

### AI & solution foundations

I can now explain and distinguish:

- Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI and Large Language Models
- Supervised Machine Learning
- Generative AI / LLM use cases
- RAG as a pattern for answering questions from specific documents
- Deterministic workflow automation versus agentic workflows
- Why solution choice should be driven by the problem type rather than by the most advanced technology

A recurring principle from this week:

> Use the simplest solution that adequately solves the business problem.

Examples:

- Historical labelled data + known outcome → often supervised ML
- Questions over approved documents → retrieval / RAG + LLM
- Fixed multi-step process → workflow automation
- Dynamic planning and tool selection → potentially an AI agent
- Summarisation or content generation → GenAI / LLM

## 2. Business Analysis for AI

I worked on several AI business scenarios and use-case canvases.

For each use case, I practised identifying:

- Business problem
- Users and stakeholders
- Desired outcome and KPIs
- Current process
- Candidate solution type
- Required data and inputs
- Human-in-the-loop responsibilities
- Risks and constraints
- Privacy and security concerns
- Success criteria
- Requirements and assumptions

An important learning point was to distinguish between:

- Functional requirements
- Data requirements
- Non-functional requirements
- Security and privacy requirements
- Governance and control requirements
- Assumptions
- Constraints
- Risks
- Project prerequisites
- Change activities

I also started using the principle that important AI requirements should be linked to an evaluation or acceptance criterion.
